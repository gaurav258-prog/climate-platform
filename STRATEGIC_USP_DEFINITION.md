## What We Collect (Minimal, Intentional)

### Required Inputs (MVP)
1. **Asset Inventory**
   - Asset name, type, location (lat/lon, country)
   - Value (EUR)
   - Sector (NACE code)
   - Why: To know "when Taxonomy changes, which of YOUR assets are affected"

2. **GHG Emissions**
   - Scope 1, 2, 3 (annual totals or by asset)
   - Why: To know carbon intensity for transition risk alerts

3. **Current Framework Status**
   - Which frameworks they report under (TCFD, Taxonomy, SEC, Basel)
   - Current versions they're compliant with
   - Why: To know "when v2.0 launches, what's your migration path"

### Optional Later (Phase 2+)
- Loan portfolio by sector (for Scope 3 financed emissions)
- Governance structure (nice-to-have for TCFD)
- Historical impacts (anecdotal, not algorithmic)

### DO NOT COLLECT (Out of scope)
- Detailed borrower data (we use sector proxies)
- Board member names
- Detailed historical losses
- Industry proprietary reserves (use public data)

## What We Output (Continuous, Real-time)

### Primary Outputs (Phase 1-2)
1. **Regulatory Change Alerts**
   - What changed, where, effective date
   - Deadline to implement
   - Estimated effort (dev hours + timeline)

2. **Personalized Impact Assessment**
   - Which of YOUR assets are affected
   - Which of YOUR systems need updates
   - Revenue/portfolio impact

3. **Risk Dashboard**
   - Climate hazard exposure (per asset, per geography)
   - Physical risk scores
   - Transition risk (stranded asset identification)

4. **Competitive Benchmarking**
   - How are peer banks responding to this change?
   - Which frameworks are competitors prioritizing?

### Secondary Outputs (Phase 3+, Optional)
- Auto-generated TCFD report template (not our focus)
- Taxonomy compliance report (not our focus)
- Form-filling assistance (not our focus)
