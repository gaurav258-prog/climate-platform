01How data moves through it

Left to right: raw feeds come in, become one governed score layer, feed the per-sector models, get frozen into regulatory filings, and are served to users through login & permissions. The gold stages are append-only — once written they can't be altered, only superseded.

02The 10 areas, in plain terms

Every table belongs to one of these. Counts, sizes and row totals are live. 🔒 marks an append-only, tamper-proof table.

Shown areas cover 145 tables; the remaining ~22 are supporting link/lookup tables. Total = 167.

03The tamper-proof core

13 tables are locked by database triggers: you can add new rows, but you can never quietly change or delete an old one. This is what makes the risk scores and the regulatory filings defensible to an auditor — the record can't be rewritten after the fact.

Two families: the golden score layer (every score is a new versioned row, old ones retired not overwritten) and every regulatory artifact (filings, submissions, report snapshots, task history) — write-once, read-many.

Why "confirmed" matters: until now the app built its tables on the fly, which quietly diverged from the written blueprint. It now builds only from migrations — the same steps produce the same 167-table database every time, from empty to working login (verified). The live database was checked table-by-table against that blueprint and matches it exactly.
