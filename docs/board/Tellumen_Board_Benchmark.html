01Executive summary

Tellumen occupies the join between two large, well-funded markets — physical-climate-risk analytics (Jupiter, First Street, Moody's RMS, S&P, MSCI, XDI) and EU regulatory reporting (Regnology, Workiva, Wolters Kluwer). Almost every incumbent leads one side and partners across the gap. Our defensible bet is the single audited pipeline from hazard → decision → filing, sharpened by two things competitors don't market: an honesty gate (publish only when calibrated to reality) and an agriculture supply-chain COGS-at-risk wedge in white space left open by Gro Intelligence's collapse.

On engineering, the picture is unusually favourable for our stage: our identity stack, data-integrity model, and audit-defensibility are ahead of the curve — precisely the things regulated buyers demand and the hardest to retrofit. What we lack is not product architecture but operational maturity and market proof: certifications, redundancy, provable reliability, scientific pedigree, brand, and scale.

Where we lead / are unique
  • The join — one engine, hazard → decision → EU filing, audited end-to-end
  • Honesty gate — r²≥0.40, "not produced yet" over a fabricated number
  • Agri COGS-at-risk — genuine white space, our clearest wedge
  • Realized exposure — real past events matched to a client's own assets
  • Identity & data integrity — SSO/SCIM/passkeys, append-only golden source, immutable snapshots
Where we lag
  • Science depth — cat-model / CMIP6 pedigree of RMS, S&P, First Street
  • Forward projections — parametric warming-shift vs CMIP6/NGFS ensembles
  • Coverage & impact-function library — MSCI / S&P scale
  • Brand, distribution, track record — incumbents' decades of FI trust
  • Operational assurance — no SOC 2, no DR/HA, no SLA, KMS/WAF, tracing
The three moves that matter most
  1. Assurance & reliability program — SOC 2 Type II (controls ~80% already built), multi-region DR/HA with a real SLA, managed KMS + edge WAF. Unlocks regulated-sector revenue that is otherwise a hard stop.
  2. Scientific credibility — CMIP6/NGFS-aligned projections, a deeper calibrated impact-function library, and independent/peer-reviewed validation. Neutralises the trust gap vs S&P / RMS / Jupiter.
  3. Own the agriculture wedge — deepen COGS-at-risk with backtests and a lighthouse customer before the Gro vacuum is contested.

04The gap, honestly

Product

We are a young challenger against decades-deep incumbents. The substantive deltas: scientific pedigree (peer-reviewed / cat-model depth), a far larger calibrated impact-function library (S&P's 270+ asset types), and coverage scale (MSCI's millions of locations). Forward projections are less of a gap than first assessed — the engine already uses real CMIP6 ensemble deltas with cited per-hazard elasticities and model-disagreement bands (not a parametric shift); what remains there is downscaled-ensemble depth and independent validation. Underneath sits the softer but decisive gap — brand, distribution, and a published validation track record that regulated buyers and their auditors trust.

Update — the validation track record is now in build. An append-only, immutable backtesting framework has been shipped: each hazard's score is tested against the independent observed catalogue (skill metrics recorded with full provenance), the honesty gate (r²≥0.40 / rank skill) is enforced as the pass/fail, and results feed model governance. It also diagnoses model-vs-test: a naive near-field storm test was saturated (nearly every coastal cell has a track), so the framework marks it not testable by this method — never a false "fail" — and the severity-appropriate test (score vs observed peak wind) then validates it. First results, honest: seismic STRONG/PASS (Spearman 0.81, AUC 0.96), storm FAIR/PASS (Spearman 0.47 on the right ruler). This converts our honesty gate from a posture into auditable proof, and directs where impact-function/coverage investment is actually needed.

Platform

We hold the controls of an enterprise platform but not yet the attestations, redundancy, and provable reliability a bank or insurer's vendor review demands. In priority order, a security review will flag: no DR/HA + single region + no SLA; no SOC 2 / ISO / pen-test; no managed KMS + no WAF; half-built observability (no tracing/SLOs/on-call); schema managed by create_all over a drifted migration chain; and — for a company whose product is model output — no model registry or drift monitoring.

06Recommendation to the Board

Fund the three-workstream program above as a single "path to enterprise-grade & category leadership" initiative, front-loading the Assurance & Reliability track (SOC 2, DR/HA + SLA, KMS/WAF). These are the lowest-effort, highest-leverage moves — none require re-architecture, most reuse controls we already have — and they convert a technically strong platform into one a regulated institution can actually buy. In parallel, protect the agriculture wedge while the category is open, and begin the scientific-credibility track that a longer sales cycle will demand.

The decision sought: approve the program and its sequencing, and the budget for (i) a SOC 2 / evidence-automation engagement and (ii) multi-region cloud + managed KMS/WAF — the two items that most directly open regulated-sector revenue.
