How to read this — and its limits (a scientist should hold us to these): ρ is the out-of-sample rank correlation of the growing-season hazard signal against the detrended observed yield (technology trend removed), n≈34 years. Headline belts carry their live backtested ρ; the rest show status from the 22-belt run. The gate is deliberately domain-calibrated (climate is one of several yield drivers), and where a driver explains yield only partly we publish a band, not a point. Held belts fail honestly for physical reasons — irrigation buffers the drought signal (US maize ρ=−0.06), or a Southern-Hemisphere harvest-year needs re-alignment (Brazil coffee ρ=−0.44). Correlation on a pre-stated mechanism is evidence for that mechanism, not proof of causation — we label everything as disclosed methodology.
