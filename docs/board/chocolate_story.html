Meet the customer

Choco Co. makes chocolate

To make chocolate you need cocoa beans 🌱 — and Choco Co. buys them from farms far away, mostly in West Africa.

But here's the problem: when it gets too hot 🌡️ or too dry 🏜️, the cocoa harvest shrinks. Fewer beans → beans cost more → chocolate costs more. Choco Co. wants to see this coming instead of being surprised.

🛰️
What WE already have

Eyes in the sky

We watch the weather over every farm on Earth — rain 🌧️, heat 🌡️, and drought 🏜️ — using satellites and official science.

And we don't just look at today. We keep 30+ years of records, so we know what "normal" looks like for each place — and when something is going wrong.

📍
What YOU give us

Just your list of places

Choco Co. only has to tell us one thing: "here are the places we buy our cocoa from."

That's it — a simple list of farms and regions. They don't need to be weather experts. We bring the science; they bring their map.

What we DO with it

Match, score… and double-check

We drop Choco Co.'s places onto our weather map and give each one a risk score, like a traffic light:

🟢 all good🟠 keep an eye on it🔴 trouble ahead
saferisky

And here's the important bit: we don't just guess. We checked our scores against 34 years of real cocoa harvests 📊 — and where the weather said "bad year", the harvests really were bad. So the score is proof, not a hunch. If we can't prove something, we say so — we never make up a number.
