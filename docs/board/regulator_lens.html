The regulator's climate lens

What if the supervisor is our customer too — regardless of what the banks under them use? They bring us the filings they already hold, and our engine independently computes where the climate risk really sits, for any FI, country, or region. We call it Tellumen Observatory: a supervisor's independent lens over the whole system. This is the move from vendor to market infrastructure.

Strategy concept · Aug 2026 · Regnology / BIS SupTech scan folded in · illustrative screens

◆ The thesis
Sell to the entity and you win one account through an 18-month cycle. Put the supervisor on the same golden source, and every entity it oversees gets pulled onto our rails to file. That flips distribution — and it's the one wedge that escapes the "annual report" trap for good.

Why it works: we re-run our engine on their book

An FI's filing is just a list of exposures and locations. The regulator brings that book into Observatory and we compute our own independent view of its climate risk — from our satellite history + backtested models. The FI can use Tellumen, a rival, or spreadsheets; our answer is unaffected. Like an independent stress-tester: "give me your book, I'll tell you the risk."

🧲

The bonus: the regulator sale pulls FIs in

When Observatory flags a gap in an FI's disclosure, that FI has to answer it — and the easiest way to answer the Tellumen lens is to adopt Tellumen. So cell 4 doesn't just stand alone; it migrates toward full two-sided over time — without waiting for the FI base first.

02How Observatory sits in the system

The supervisor sits on the shared golden source; independent satellite data flows in to cross-check filings; and over time the mandate pulls entities onto the same rails.

03The wedge only we have

Don't sell regulators reporting plumbing — the incumbents own that pipe. Sell them the thing no one else can: an independent physical-risk lens that checks whether a filing is actually true, against satellite ground truth and our backtested models.

04Where this sits vs the incumbents

We checked. Regnology's Supervisory Hub and the BIS climate prototypes are all built on submitted data — none brings independent satellite ground truth to challenge a filing. The white space is real.

What they do today

  • Regnology Supervisory Hub (EBA, ECB, PRA, BaFin, ACPR, Banco de España…) — collect, validate, aggregate, run analytics & stress on submitted data. Its climate product helps FIs produce disclosures, not supervisors test them.
  • BIS Project Gaia — LLMs extract climate metrics from corporate disclosures; no earth-observation, no ground truth.
  • BIS Project Ellipse — analytics & early-warning over collected reporting data.

What none of them do

Independently model the physical risk behind a filing — from satellite ground truth + backtested attribution, across the whole supervised population, regardless of the FI's vendor or the filing's origin. That is Observatory.

Confirmed against Regnology RSH, BIS Gaia / Ellipse, and NGFS & SUERF papers naming the physical-climate data gap.

Two honest cautions from the scan: (1) The gap is loudly documented — NGFS, BIS and academics all name the satellite/EO gap, so this is a lead, not a moat: move before the concept is claimed. (2) Regnology owns the supervisor pipes. A regulator's path of least resistance is an EO feed into their existing hub — so the real choice is embed vs compete: feeding Observatory's lens into an RSH-style platform may beat selling a second one. Defensibility lives in the independent-lens framing + backtested attribution + population-scale coverage, not the raw satellite data, which is commoditising.

06The honest risks

This is a bigger, slower bet than the FI product. Four things make it hard — each has a real answer.

1 · Selling to regulators is brutal

Tiny buyer set, public procurement, political, multi-year. Harder GTM than FIs, with the same chicken-and-egg.

Answer: enter via an innovation-hub / sandbox pilot, not a tender. Validate cheap before committing.

2 · Neutrality & conflict

If the entity and its supervisor both pay us, the regulator must trust we're not captured.

Answer: our independent EO ground truth means we're not just re-serving the bank's numbers — plus explicit governance walls.

3 · The incumbent owns the pipe

Regnology already runs the central-bank reporting rails (and is a potential channel for us).

Answer: partner, don't rebuild. Ride their rails; supply the climate analytical + independent-lens layer on top.

4 · We're pre-lighthouse

No central bank buys from a vendor with zero production customers, no SOC 2, no peer-reviewed validation.

Answer: this is precisely why it's Phase 3 — it rides on the FI base and credibility gates, not ahead of them.
