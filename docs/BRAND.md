# Tellumen — Brand Book (v1)

*Light on the Earth.*

## 1. The name
**Tellumen** — *Tellus* (Latin: Earth) + *lumen* (Latin: light) = **"light on the Earth."**
One word, no space. A buried **"tell"** carries a second meaning: the platform *tells* you
what the Earth is showing.

- **Tagline:** Light on the Earth.
- **Category descriptor (when needed):** climate & physical-risk intelligence.
- **Lexicon it unlocks:** *the Tellumen Score* (the golden source), *Tellumen Earth* (the map).

Chosen because the root *Tellus* means **Earth**, not "climate" — deliberately broad, so the
brand survives expansion from climate into nature / physical-asset risk without dating.

## 2. Positioning
- **Aperture — broad.** Earth / physical-risk, not welded to the word "climate."
- **Structure — standalone.** Its own identity; a category-defining "golden source."
- **Tone — modern deep-tech + evocative,** carrying quiet **source-of-truth authority**: the
  one number a bank, an insurer, an agribusiness *and* a regulator all read.

## 3. The wordmark
`Tellumen` set as **one word**, with the final five letters — **lumen** — carrying the accent
(the light is lit):
- On dark grounds: "lumen" in **Sky #8fd0ff**, "Tel" in the near-white ink.
- On light grounds: "lumen" in **Signal Blue #0071e3**, "Tel" in Ink #1d1d1f.

## 4. The mark
A **hexagon** — the H3 cell the engine tessellates the Earth into (the heritage of the whole
naming exploration) — holding a **rising lumen** (a radial light with a bright core). Self-
contained colours so it reads on light or dark. Files: `ui/public/favicon.svg` and the React
component `ui/src/components/BrandMark.jsx`.

Clear space ≥ the hexagon's height on all sides. Minimum size 20 px. Never recolour, rotate,
or stretch the mark.

## 6. How we got here (naming decision log)
1. **Brief:** broad, standalone, modern deep-tech + evocative, must read as a source of truth
   to institutional buyers.
2. **Territory:** tessellation / tiled-Earth — it encodes the H3 hexagonal grid the engine runs on.
3. **Tessera** — clicked (each H3 cell is a *tessera*; the mosaic is the global picture) — but
   trademark-contested (Tessera Therapeutics; Xperi/Tessera semiconductor IP).
4. **Terrazzo** — loved (*terra* + a mosaic of set chips) — but a generic flooring word: `.com`
   owned by the trade, `terrazzo.app` taken, a weak trademark path.
5. **Ownability filter** (each checked, each collided):
   - *Hexis* — registered EU trademark + multiple software firms.
   - *Cadastra* — an established 20-year data/marketing company owns `cadastra.com`.
   - *Sidera* — Sidera Software **and** Sidera Space & Defence (adjacent to us).
   - *Terralumen* — `terralumen.com` taken; `terralumen.de` is a **solar** company (adjacent);
     *TeraLumen* (AI imaging) is a homophone.
   - *LumenTerra* — crowded in both orders; carries the Terra/Luna crypto association.
6. **Coined siblings:** *Tellumen* (Tellus + lumen) and *Petralumen* (Petra + lumen).
7. **Selected — Tellumen.** Same "light on the Earth" meaning as Terralumen, but a true coinage:
   the scan found **no company, product, or trademark** collision — an ownable namespace.

## 7. Availability & legal (status)
A web scan found no company / product / trademark collision on **Tellumen** — the cleanest
result in the whole exploration. **Before any spend**, complete formal clearance: a
`tellumen.com` WHOIS check and a USPTO / EUIPO search in **Class 42 (SaaS)** and **Class 9**,
ideally via a trademark attorney. *(A web scan is not legal clearance.)*

## 8. Usage — do / don't
**Do:** write it as one word, *Tellumen*; keep the two-tone (accent the "lumen"); give the mark
clear space; pair with *"Light on the Earth."*
**Don't:** add a space (*Tel lumen*); hyphenate; recolour, rotate or stretch the mark; set the
wordmark on a low-contrast ground.
