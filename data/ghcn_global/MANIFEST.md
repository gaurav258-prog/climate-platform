# GHCN-Daily global station extremes (Africa, Asia, Latin America, Oceania)
- Source: NOAA NCEI Access Data Service https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1 (dataset daily-summaries, TMIN/TMAX/PRCP, 1991-01-01..2020-12-31); inventory/station list https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/ (ghcnd-inventory.txt, ghcnd-stations.txt, in data/ghcn/). Public domain, no credentials.
- Acquired 2026-09-21 by scripts/ingest_ghcn_global.py (4 threads, 0.5 s pause per request). Output: extremes.json.
- Selection: 1 station per 1 degree box, >=20 complete years (>=330 daily values of each element); hash-ordered, up to 5 candidates per box.
- **Extended 2026-09-23**: raised the box cap from 150 to Africa's/Latin America's full candidate pool (305/285) — the honest next step after a 150-box Africa sample found zero qualifying stations was to search the REST of the real candidate pool, never to relax the completeness rule to manufacture a result. Asia/Oceania picked up the same higher cap incidentally (not their full pools — 1170/267 candidates vs 310/267 boxes tried).
- Counts: **africa 0** (0/305 candidate boxes — searched the FULL pool, genuinely no station meets the completeness rule; a real data-sparsity finding, not a code gap), asia 100 (was 48), latin_america_caribbean 6 (was 3, still thin — 285 candidates all tried), oceania 202 (was 114) — **total 308** (was 165) from all 1,167 candidate boxes tried across Africa+LatAm, and 310/1170 + 267/267 for Asia/Oceania.
- Caveats: Africa's TMIN/TMAX/PRCP completeness (>=330/365 days for >=20 years) is genuinely not met by any station in the full candidate pool searched — GHCN-Daily's African network has real reporting gaps, not a search-depth artefact. Latin America stays thin (6 stations) even at full pool. Oceania is still mostly Australia/NZ. Asia's pool is 1170 candidates; only 310 tried (not exhaustive — a full Asia search is a larger, separate network-bound run). Stations are mostly airports/synoptic sites, so a coastal/urban siting bias applies.
